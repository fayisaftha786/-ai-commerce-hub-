      OFFICIAL PLATFORM WORKFLOW

      Content Rewards submission guide

      Use the matching route from the campaign brief. This guide is a checklist; ClipReward Studio does not submit clips or verify approvals.

      ↗
      Before you post: the exact social account you will publish from must show Connected inside Content Rewards; a Whop sign-in alone is not enough. The official Instagram connection guide says Business only, but Creator Terms §14.1 and Privacy Policy §4.1 allow Professional/Business or Creator via OAuth. Try your current Creator account first; ask Support about any rejection before switching. Connection guide · Terms · Privacy Policy.
      ROUTE A · MOST CAMPAIGNS

      Post first, then submit the link

      1. Join the campaign, or apply and wait until the brand approves you.
      2. Read the brief. Use permitted footage/audio, place a clear disclosure at the beginning, and do not use Collab/co-posting unless the brief allows it.
      3. Publish the clip publicly from the exact account connected and verified in Content Rewards.
      4. Open that campaign, paste the public post link and submit it while the post is less than 30 minutes old.

      Content Rewards checks: the posting account is linked, the post has not already been submitted to this campaign, and it was posted less than 30 minutes ago.

      Official standard submission steps ↗
      ROUTE B · PRE-APPROVAL CAMPAIGNS

      Get the draft approved before posting

      1. Use Submit clip to upload your draft file before publishing it.
      2. Choose the connected account you intend to post from and wait for the campaign team to approve the draft or request changes.
      3. After approval, publish from one of the accounts approved on that draft.
      4. Submit the live public post link while the post is less than 30 minutes old, just like the standard route.

      A draft approval is not the same as approval of the final live submission. The posted clip is reviewed again.

      Official pre-approval instructions ↗
      After submission

      The submission appears as Pending while the campaign team reviews it. Nothing is approved automatically. CPM, per-post, retainer and pool campaigns have different earning and payout schedules. A pool is a shared allocation, not a guaranteed amount for each creator; check the campaign formula and official terms instead of treating this local log as earnings data.
